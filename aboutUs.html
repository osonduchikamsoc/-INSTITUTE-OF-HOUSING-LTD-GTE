NATIONAL ENGAGEMENT

Championing Housing Reform at the National Level

In November 2024, the Institute of Housing Administration and Management Ltd/GTE formally reached out to the Federal Ministry of Housing and Urban Development to present its vision for strengthening professional standards across Nigeria's housing sector, particularly in the administration of rents, and the sale of buildings and land.

Our submission outlined a clear objective: to build an enabling environment where housing transactions are handled with accountability and professionalism, and to grow the Institute into a globally recognized body — following the model of the Chartered Institute of Housing in London, which today counts over forty-five thousand members and continues to shape housing policy worldwide.

This engagement reflects our ongoing commitment to collaborating with government bodies, regulators, and industry stakeholders to raise the standard of housing administration across the country.

Government Partnership Engaging directly with the Federal Ministry of Housing and Urban Development. Global Standard Working toward the professional benchmark set by institutions like the Chartered Institute of Housing, London.
